Modern Slavery Statement

Our commitment to preventing modern slavery and human trafficking in our business and supply chains.

Financial Year Ending 2026 — Published May 2026

Introduction

This statement is made pursuant to section 54(1) of the Modern Slavery Act 2015 and constitutes Kingscote Haulage Ltd's Modern Slavery and Human Trafficking Statement for the financial year ending 2026.

Kingscote Haulage Ltd is a subsidiary of the Morrisroe Group and operates as an environmental haulage and waste management specialist, providing services to construction, civil engineering and industrial clients across London and the South East of England.

Our Organisation and Supply Chains

Kingscote Haulage Ltd employs professional drivers, logistics coordinators and operations staff. Our primary activities involve the movement of construction materials, waste and aggregates using our own fleet of Euro VI compliant Scania tipper vehicles.

Our supply chains include:

  • Fuel and vehicle parts suppliers.
  • Subcontract hauliers and plant hire companies.
  • Waste disposal and treatment facilities.
  • Fleet maintenance and servicing contractors.
  • Uniform, PPE and safety equipment suppliers.

Our Policies

We are committed to acting ethically and with integrity across all our operations. Our relevant policies include:

  • Whistleblowing Policy: providing confidential channels for employees and supply chain partners to raise concerns about potential modern slavery without fear of retaliation.
  • Supplier Code of Conduct: setting out our expectations of suppliers regarding labour rights, ethical sourcing and compliance with applicable law.
  • Recruitment Policy: ensuring all workers are employed on legal terms, with identity and right-to-work checks carried out for all employees and temporary workers.
  • Equal Opportunities Policy: promoting fair treatment and diversity across our workforce.

Due Diligence

We take the following steps to identify and address the risk of modern slavery in our operations and supply chains:

  • All employees undergo right-to-work verification prior to engagement.
  • Subcontractor and supplier assessments include questions relating to labour standards and ethical practices.
  • We require all subcontract hauliers to hold valid operators' licences and to confirm compliance with driver working time regulations.
  • Site visits and periodic reviews of key suppliers are conducted to assess working conditions.

Risk Assessment

We recognise that the construction and haulage sectors carry a higher-than-average risk of labour exploitation, particularly in relation to:

  • Subcontract and agency labour arrangements.
  • Night-shift and long-distance driving operations.
  • Supply chains extending into countries with weaker labour protections.

We consider our direct workforce risk to be low, given our employment model and verification procedures. We continue to assess risk within our subcontractor and supplier base.

Training and Awareness

Our management team and relevant operational staff receive awareness training on modern slavery indicators. Training is reviewed annually and updated in line with changes to legislation and best practice guidance.

Key Performance Indicators

We monitor our progress through the following indicators:

  • Percentage of key suppliers assessed against our Supplier Code of Conduct.
  • Number of concerns raised via whistleblowing channels and actions taken.
  • Completion rate of modern slavery awareness training among relevant staff.

This statement has been approved by the Board of Directors of Kingscote Haulage Ltd.
