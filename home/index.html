Our Services

Site Clearance & Muck Away

We provide a range of solutions to prepare your site for construction. These include removing materials classified as hazardous, which may harm the environment or human health and require an extra duty of care. Clean inert waste, which does not come with such risks, as well as non-hazardous materials that may have low levels of contamination are also efficiently removed.

Our team carry out necessary testing to classify materials according to each site's requirements and can subsequently record and validate the removal of contaminated materials.

Learn more →

Recycled Aggregates

Thanks to our network of third-party treatment facilities and recycling centres, we can arrange for the disposal and processing of inert waste and non-hazardous materials for use as recycled aggregate.

Our team provides crushing and screening services to produce recycled aggregates for future use, grading them to specifications including 6F1, 6F5 and Types 1 to 3, suitable for individual site requirements. This helps reduce the amount of waste being disposed of in landfills, cutting both costs and environmental impact.

Waste Disposal

A variety of skips and roll-on, roll-off containers, which come in larger sizes for bigger waste volumes, can be supplied to sites to assist with waste removal. Container sizes range from six to 40 cubic yards, providing a flexible solution for projects of any scale and duration.

About Kingscote

Reliable and Responsible Waste Management Services

Kingscote Haulage was established to provide efficient environmental haulage and waste management solutions to Morrisroe operations through improved logistics and increased control. What began as an internal solution has grown into a specialist haulage business serving a broad external client base.

Our Euro VI compliant Scania tipper fleet and specialist operations team deliver a level of service and environmental accountability that sets us apart.
