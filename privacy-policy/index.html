Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data.

Last updated: May 2026

Who We Are

Kingscote Haulage Ltd is the data controller responsible for your personal data. We are registered in England and Wales and our registered address is Units 3–4 Oaks Court, Warwick Road, Borehamwood, Hertfordshire, WD6 1GS.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at info@kingscotehaulage.co.uk or by telephone on +44 (0)20 8731 4000.

What Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal data when you use this website:

  • Contact form submissions: your first and last name, email address, phone number, company name and project details you provide when submitting an enquiry.
  • Usage data: your IP address, browser type, referring pages and pages visited, collected automatically via server logs.
  • Cookies and local storage: small data files stored on your device. See our Cookies Policy for full details.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide the information or services you have requested.
  • Contact you regarding your project or quote.
  • Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Improve the performance and content of our website.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

  • Legitimate interests: responding to business enquiries and managing our operations.
  • Consent: where you have opted in to the use of non-essential cookies.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is required to comply with a legal or regulatory requirement.

Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • Our parent company, the Morrisroe Group, where relevant to your enquiry.
  • IT service providers and hosting partners who process data on our behalf under appropriate data processing agreements.
  • Google LLC, if you consent to the use of Google Maps on our contact page. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details of how they process data.
  • Regulatory authorities, law enforcement or other parties where required by law.

How Long We Keep Your Data

Enquiry data submitted via our contact form is retained for up to two years from the date of submission, after which it is securely deleted unless a contractual relationship has been established.

Server log data is retained for up to 90 days for security and performance monitoring purposes.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: to request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: to request deletion of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restrict processing: to request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability: to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@kingscotehaulage.co.uk. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or destruction. All data transmitted via our website is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of this website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
